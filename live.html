Storm planner

This page scores 900 made-up assets with the same gradient-boosting model that was trained in Python and tested on held-out years. Move a region's storm slider and the model re-scores every asset. The planner then shows where a fixed number of spares should go.

Fill rate comes from 300 simulated years drawn from the model's risk scores. Both policies get the same total spares.
